EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (the "Company") and the undersigned Employee (the "Employee").

1. EFFECTIVE DATE
Effective Date: March 1, 2026. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until terminated as provided herein.

2. POSITION AND DUTIES
The Employee shall serve as Senior Software Engineer and agrees to perform duties assigned by the Company in good faith.

3. COMPENSATION
The Company shall pay the Employee a monthly salary of INR 110,000, payable by the 5th of each following month. The Employee agrees to submit a monthly timesheet by the 5th of each month.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Employee agrees to keep confidential all proprietary information of the Company, both during and after employment. This confidentiality obligation is perpetual and survives termination of this Agreement.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All inventions, work product, and intellectual property created by the Employee during the course of employment shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company. This assignment is irrevocable.

6. NON-COMPETE
The Employee shall not engage in any competing business within 18 months after termination of this Agreement, within India and any country where the Company operates.

7. TERMINATION
The Company may terminate this Agreement without prior notice in cases of misconduct or at the Company's sole discretion for underperformance. In all other cases, either party may terminate this Agreement with 15 days written notice.

8. LIABILITY
The Company's liability under this Agreement shall be unlimited liability for breaches of confidentiality by the Employee.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall be resolved through arbitration in Bangalore, India, in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

11. DATA PROTECTION
The Employee's personal data shall be processed in accordance with the Company's privacy policy and applicable data protection law.

12. STOCK OPTIONS
The Employee shall be eligible for stock options under the Company's ESOP scheme, subject to a 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff.
