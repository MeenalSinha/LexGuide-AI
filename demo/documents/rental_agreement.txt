RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is made between Mr. Ramesh Gupta (the "Landlord") and the Tenant identified below.

1. EFFECTIVE DATE
Effective Date: February 1, 2026. This Agreement shall run for a period of 11 months from the Effective Date.

2. RENT
The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of INR 22,000, due within 5 days of the start of each month. A security deposit of INR 66,000 is payable before occupancy.

3. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for minor repairs up to INR 2,000 per occurrence. The Landlord shall be responsible for major structural repairs.

4. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice. The Landlord may terminate immediately without notice in case of non-payment of rent for 2 consecutive months.

5. RENEWAL
This Agreement may be renewed for a further term upon mutual written consent, at a rent to be mutually agreed, no later than 30 days before expiry.

6. RESTRICTIONS
The Tenant shall not sublet the premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts in the city where the premises are located.
