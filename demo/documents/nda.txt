MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is between NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("Disclosing Party") and the Receiving Party identified in the signature block.

1. EFFECTIVE DATE
Effective Date: January 1, 2026.

2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" means any non-public business, technical, or financial information disclosed by either party.

3. OBLIGATIONS
The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party and shall use it solely for the purpose of evaluating a potential business relationship. The Receiving Party agrees to return or destroy all Confidential Information within 30 days of a written request.

4. TERM
This Agreement shall remain in effect for 2 years from the Effective Date, and the confidentiality obligations shall survive for an additional 3 years after termination.

5. EXCLUSIONS
This Agreement does not apply to information that is publicly available, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party.

6. REMEDIES
The parties agree that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm, entitling the non-breaching party to seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, with disputes subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Bangalore.
